Prospective benchmark

5,809 ongoing trials, open for forecasts

The benchmark covers the eligible ongoing trials of companies listed in the United States, including trials without an announced readout date. TrialAugur's forecasts for every trial were recorded before any outcome was disclosed and stay fixed. The trial list is public so that any method can be scored on the same trials as outcomes are adjudicated.

How the cohort was built

ClinicalTrials.gov, queried in October 2026

  1. 121,961records with an ongoing status: recruiting, not yet recruiting, active and not recruiting, or enrolling by invitation
  2. 10,607with a lead sponsor or collaborator listed on the NYSE, Nasdaq, NYSE American or Cboe, including American depositary receipts and subsidiaries mapped to their listed parent
  3. 8,686interventional studies
  4. 7,418with a specified phase, including early phase 1
  5. 6,855not enrolling healthy volunteers
  6. 5,983after removing 872 trials with a disclosed final result or an interim stop, found by web search of the 1,736 trials whose primary completion date had passed or that had posted results
  7. 5,809after excluding 86 trials whose dossiers listed no intervention, 20 in the development cohort, 25 in the retrospective test cohort and 43 of the initial prospective cohort whose readouts had been disclosed before the forecasts were recorded

What is recorded

  • Forecasts. The two TrialAugur experts, their rank ensemble and a Claude Opus 5.5 zero shot comparator, recorded before outcome disclosure. Forecast values and evidence dossiers are not published on this site.
  • Outcomes. Adjudicated under the same criteria as the initial prospective cohort, each with its supporting source; disclosures that support neither label stay unresolved.
  • Scoring. Methods are compared on shared eligible trials by ROC AUC, average precision and classification errors at 0.5, and by Brier score and expected calibration error for probability forecasts, with uncertainty, forecast coverage and the numbers of adjudicated, unresolved and pending trials.
  • Your forecasts. Submit for any subset of trials while they are pending; see Contribute.
