Open benchmark

Submit your forecasts

Every question here is a future trial readout, so the answer cannot be in any training set. We provide the readouts and the adjudicated labels; the forecasts can come from anyone. Submit while trials are pending and you are scored on exactly the trials that were unresolved at your submission time.

What to submit

One CSV per model, one row per trial you want scored.

Rules

  • Lock before readout. Only forecasts submitted while a trial is still pending are scored for that trial. For pull requests the commit time is the lock time.
  • No edits after readout. You may update forecasts for pending trials; a trial's forecast is frozen at its last value before readout.
  • Any evidence dated before readout is allowed. Literature, registries, filings, web search: anything, as long as it predates the readout.
  • Coverage is flexible. Forecast any subset of pending trials; metrics are reported with the number of trials covered.

How outcomes are labelled

A trial is a success when the disclosure explicitly states that the prespecified primary endpoint was met or that the next development phase will start. It is a failure when the disclosure reports a missed primary endpoint, unacceptable safety or tolerability, or discontinuation of the trial. Disclosures without enough evidence for either label stay unresolved and are not scored. Every label links its primary source.

Scoring

  • PR AUC with success as the positive class (primary), and ROC AUC
  • Specificity and NPV at 0.5: how many failures you catch and how often your failure calls are right
  • Brier score and balanced accuracy at 0.5

TrialAugur's locked forecasts and the two zero shot comparators are scored under the same rules and appear on the Results page as reference baselines.

How to submit

  • Pull request (preferred): add your CSV as submissions/<model_name>.csv in the site repository.
  • GitHub issue: open an issue on the tracker with your CSV attached.

Questions and label disputes

Open an issue on the tracker. Disputed labels are reviewed again against the linked evidence, and revisions are recorded.
