Open benchmark

Submit your forecasts

Every question here is a future trial readout, so the answer cannot be in any training set. Forecast any of the 5,809 benchmark trials while it is pending. Outcomes are adjudicated under the rules below, and each method is scored on the trials it forecast before their readouts.

One CSV per model, one row per trial you want scored.

Rules

  • Record before readout. Only forecasts submitted while a trial is still pending are scored for that trial. The submission timestamp is the time the forecast was recorded; for pull requests this is the commit time.
  • No edits after readout. You may update forecasts for pending trials; a trial's forecast is frozen at its last value before readout.
  • Any evidence dated before readout is allowed. Literature, registries, filings, web search: anything, as long as it predates the readout.
  • Coverage is flexible. Forecast any subset of pending trials; metrics are reported with the number of trials covered.

How outcomes are labelled

A trial is a success when the disclosure explicitly reports that the prespecified primary endpoint was met, or clearly announces the start of the next development phase. A missed primary endpoint is a failure unless the sponsor explicitly announces the start of the next phase; general development plans, intentions to discuss development with regulators and conditional statements do not count as advancement. Unacceptable safety or tolerability and discontinuation of the trial are also failures. Disclosures that support neither label stay unresolved and are not scored. Every label links its primary source, and sponsor framing does not change the label.

Scoring

  • ROC AUC and average precision (AP), with success as the positive class
  • Classification errors at 0.5: failures identified (specificity and NPV) and successful trials classified as failures
  • Brier score and expected calibration error for probability forecasts

Methods are compared on shared eligible trials, with uncertainty estimates, forecast coverage and the numbers of adjudicated, unresolved and pending trials. TrialAugur's recorded forecasts are scored under the same rules; see the Results page for the initial prospective comparison.

How to submit

  • Pull request (preferred): add your CSV as submissions/<model_name>.csv in the site repository.
  • GitHub issue: open an issue on the tracker with your CSV attached.

Questions and label disputes

Open an issue on the tracker. Disputed labels are reviewed again against the linked evidence, and revisions are recorded.
