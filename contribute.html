Contribute predictions

TrialAugur is a FutureX-style live benchmark: the questions are real upcoming trial readouts, so answers cannot exist in any training set. We provide the readouts and the adjudicated labels; the predictions come from the community. Submit while trials are pending, and your model is scored on exactly the trials that were unresolved at your submission time.

What to submit

One CSV per model, one row per trial you want scored:

ColumnTypeDescription
nct_idstringTrial identifier from the predictions table (e.g. NCT05499013)
p_successfloat 0–1Predicted probability that the trial reads out as a success
model_namestringShort stable name for your method (also the file name)
generated_atISO dateWhen the predictions were produced

Rules

  • Lock before readout. Only predictions submitted while a trial is still pending are scored for that trial. The submission timestamp is the lock; for pull requests, the commit time is authoritative.
  • No post-hoc edits. You may update predictions for trials that are still pending; predictions for reported trials are frozen at their last pre-readout value.
  • Any evidence dated before readout is fair game. Build your features from literature, registries, filings — anything, as long as the information predates the readout.
  • Coverage is flexible. Predict any subset of pending trials; metrics are reported alongside the number of trials covered.

How to submit

  • Pull request (preferred): add your CSV as submissions/<model_name>.csv in the site repository. The commit timestamp becomes your lock time.
  • GitHub issue: open an issue on the tracker with your CSV attached if you prefer not to open a PR.

Scoring

After each adjudication round, submissions are evaluated on the trials that were pending at their lock time and have since been adjudicated success or fail (trials under review are excluded until resolved):

  • ROC–AUC and Brier score over covered trials
  • Accuracy at the 0.5 threshold
  • High-confidence accuracy — accuracy on the top 20% / 30% of predictions ranked by |p − 0.5|

Our own locked ensemble (Predictions) is scored under identical rules and serves as the reference baseline. A public leaderboard will appear here once external submissions arrive.

Questions

Open an issue on the tracker — including for disagreements with an adjudicated label. Label disputes are re-reviewed against the linked evidence and revisions are recorded.
