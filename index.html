How it works

1 · WE PUBLISH

Frozen dossiers & locked predictions

Each trial ships with a 53-variable evidence dossier (design, sponsor profile, mechanism, target biology, prior-phase results — assembled before readout) and our ensemble's probability of success, frozen and timestamped. Registry result fields are excluded to prevent leakage.

2 · WE ADJUDICATE

Spin-aware readout labels

Roughly every two weeks, newly reported readouts are labeled success / fail by evidence-based review rather than press-release framing. Every call links its primary evidence and carries an adjudication note; ambiguous readouts stay under review.

3 · YOU CONTRIBUTE

Community predictions

We provide the readouts and the adjudication — the models come from you. Run your method on features built from sources dated before readout, submit timestamped predictions while trials are still pending, and get scored against the same labels. How to contribute →

Why adjudication has to be spin-aware

Sponsor communications are systematically optimistic: in recent catalyst samples ~80% of company press releases frame results positively, yet only a minority explicitly state that the primary endpoint was met — and registry result posting lags by months to years. Automated answer-scraping would inflate success rates, so outcomes here are adjudicated from primary evidence, and each label can be audited via its linked source.
