How it works

01

Collect

TrialCurator, evidence agent

Turns a ClinicalTrials.gov identifier into a fixed schema dossier of 33 variables drawn from ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed, Open Targets, openFDA, prespecified web sources and company disclosures. Missing evidence stays marked as missing.

02

Forecast

TrialAugur, predictor

A GPT-5 reasoning expert, an embedding expert (TabPFN-3) and a fine tuned Qwen3-235B expert each estimate the probability of success. A prespecified simple rank ensemble averages their percentile ranks.

03

Adjudicate

Prespecified evidentiary rules

Success needs an explicit statement that the primary endpoint was met or that the next phase will start. A missed primary endpoint, unacceptable safety or discontinuation is failure. Anything less stays unresolved.

31%of 83 failed trials were announced in a positive tone

Why labels cannot come from press releases

Among 83 failures that read out between June 2025 and March 2026, 26 were framed positively. The most common pattern was a missed primary endpoint with another finding made the headline, followed by emphasis on a subgroup, on response rates or biomarkers, or on secondary endpoints.

Every label on this site links its primary source, and disclosures without enough evidence remain unresolved.
