Positive framing of unsuccessful trials

Framing was judged by whether the dominant message of the disclosure (title, subtitle and conclusion) was favorable. Positive framing did not necessarily correspond to success under the outcome criteria. For example, the final update of the phase 1b study of NXP800 in platinum resistant ovarian cancer (NCT05226507) announced that development in ovarian cancer would not be pursued, while presenting two unconfirmed partial responses and three cases of stable disease among 13 evaluable patients as evidence of biological activity.

Discussion

The main points of the Discussion of the manuscript, with links to the results above.

Evidence and labels

With the same GPT-5 model, the full TrialCurator dossier improved discrimination over registry information alone, and LLM costs stayed below US$1 per trial. Because some disclosures framed unsuccessful trials positively, labels rest on the reported endpoint results and development decisions, with the same rules whatever the framing.

Evidence construction →

Ranking and classification errors

The LLM comparators had higher discrimination point estimates and lower Brier scores than either expert. At 0.5 the reasoning expert identified 27 of the 29 failures but flagged many successful trials, so its success predictions were selective. A threshold suited to choosing trials for further review may not suit decisions about continuing a programme; the rank score of the ensemble supports relative prioritization.

Prospective evaluation →

Information beyond public expectations

Many correct comparator failure forecasts referred to earlier public assessments, while the ensemble also identified failures for which no such assessment was retrieved. The audit records documented expectations and cited sources; it cannot show that an outcome was unanticipated or that a source caused a forecast.

Financial responses

Failures identified by the ensemble but predicted to succeed by at least one comparator were followed by more negative abnormal returns than failures identified by both comparators. Returns also depend on the size of price moves and on company exposure, and negative returns after some successful readouts show that a binary outcome does not capture all information relevant to valuation.

Limits of the initial comparison

The 69 adjudicable outcomes come from trials selected for anticipated readouts, which limits the precision of comparisons across phases, and disclosure timing can change the evaluated population. Success includes advancement to a specified milestone, so performance may partly reflect anticipation of development decisions. Paired forecasts recorded before disclosure on common evidence snapshots would test whether improvements from model updates persist prospectively.

The benchmark

The 5,809 ongoing trials extend evaluation beyond trials selected for imminent readouts, with forecasts that stay fixed as outcomes accumulate. The cohort is restricted to eligible trials of US listed companies; other sponsor populations would test generalizability more broadly.
